01 / THE STARTING POINT

The business behind the brief.

The engagement called for a coordinated ecommerce effort: advertising needed to connect with the storefront and with the relationship after a customer’s first visit or purchase.

02 / THE WORK

What the team brought together.

01

Overhaul acquisition

Reworked the Meta and Google advertising platforms with a focus on structure and efficient execution.

02

Improve the shopping journey

Optimized the Shopify experience and worked on average order value and customer lifetime value.

03

Build the retention system

Rebuilt welcome, abandonment, winback and post-purchase flows, with campaign planning across promotions, launches and loyalty.

03 / THE OUTCOME

What the record shows.

The deck documents the scope across paid media, Shopify and email. It says average order value and lifetime value improved but provides no numerical baseline or lift; no percentage increase is invented here.

THE OPERATING LESSON

Acquisition becomes more useful when the website and customer lifecycle support it. The work reflects a team looking across the commercial journey.
