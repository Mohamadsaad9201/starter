01 / THE STARTING POINT

The business behind the brief.

The supplied brief described uneven traffic and a need to develop more direct customer relationships. It proposed capturing local dining demand while improving the route from discovery to ordering.

02 / THE WORK

What the team brought together.

01

Delivered website experience

The live site connects menu browsing with pickup, delivery, catering, the restaurant story and location information.

02

Proposed acquisition plan

The Phase 1 blueprint proposes gluten-free search, local restaurant and chicken search, and Google Business Profile activity.

03

Proposed measurement foundation

The plan includes conversion tracking, call tracking, GA4, UTM parameters and recurring performance reviews.

03 / THE OUTCOME

What the record shows.

The website is available to inspect. The deck is a proposed marketing blueprint; its launch schedule, budgets and walk-in growth target are not campaign results. No walk-in, sales or conversion uplift is claimed.

THE OPERATING LESSON

Wally and Stephen’s restaurant story is part of the customer experience. The website makes it easier to understand the business and choose the next action.
