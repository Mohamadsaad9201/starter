01 / THE STARTING POINT

The business behind the brief.

The starting point was a business reporting $20,000 in monthly revenue. More demand would only be valuable if the business could capture inquiries and support the next stage of growth.

02 / THE WORK

What the team brought together.

01

Build the acquisition engine

Launched and optimized Google Search campaigns from the ground up.

02

Strengthen local discovery

Developed the Google Business Profile and local map presence alongside search advertising.

03

Connect demand to intake

Built a customer-intake plan and supported the business through successive growth phases.

03 / THE OUTCOME

What the record shows.

The agency deck reports monthly revenue moving from $20,000 to more than $400,000. This describes the business’s reported revenue progression; it does not establish how much incremental revenue advertising alone caused.

THE OPERATING LESSON

The work connected acquisition with what happened after a customer reached out. That operating perspective is the experience Impackt Media brings to agent-supported execution.
