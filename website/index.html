THE PROBLEM BEHIND THE CHANNEL

Traffic cannot fix an unhelpful destination.

A customer who lands on your site still needs to understand the product, trust the details and know what to do next. Website improvements should serve that decision.

A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before your agent gets to work.

Is this a complete website builder?

This workflow improves existing websites. It is not a promise to rebuild or redesign an entire site automatically.

Why distinguish saved and verified?

A platform can accept a change while a theme, plugin or publication step affects what customers see. Live-page verification checks the rendered result separately.

Can changes be reversed?

Supported changes retain rollback information. Availability depends on the change type and platform; review the recorded state before reversing anything.
