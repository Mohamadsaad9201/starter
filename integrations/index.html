Connection questions

Does connecting a source authorize every action?

No. Read access, write permissions, review decisions and supported execution are separate. The workspace should reflect the account’s actual capabilities.

Can clients manage credentials?

Client Viewer and Reviewer roles use a scoped workspace. Agency administrators manage credentials, platform permissions and execution.
