A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before your agent gets to work.

Is an AI citation the same as a website visit?

No. Citations, clicks, orders and profit are different outcomes. A citation may not lead to a click, and an observed change is not proof of causation.

Can I read the answer behind a recommendation?

Yes, recorded checks and available evidence can be inspected in the agency workspace. Access depends on your assigned workspace role.

Does special AI markup guarantee visibility?

No. Impackt Media focuses on useful, accessible content and observed evidence rather than presenting special markup as a universal visibility shortcut.
