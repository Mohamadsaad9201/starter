Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Impackt Media ("we", "us") helps businesses see how their marketing is performing and fix what is holding them back. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, used to sign you in.
  • Business details you enter: business name, website, description, voice and the leads you add.
  • Data from services you connect: only after you click Connect and approve access. Depending on what you connect, this can include Google Search Console performance data (clicks, impressions, queries, pages), Google Analytics reports, your store's products, pages, files, navigation and order totals (Shopify), page settings (Webflow) and posts and pages (WordPress).
  • Website content: the public pages of the website you add, which we read to find SEO problems.

How we use it

Only to provide Impackt Media to you: showing your Daily Pulse, finding issues on your site, proposing fixes, applying the fixes you approve, tracking how AI assistants describe your business, drafting content you ask for, answering questions in the AI assistant, and publishing articles you explicitly approve. Assistant questions and replies are saved with your business workspace so you can continue the conversation. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed below.

Google user data

Impackt Media's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to show your search and analytics performance inside Impackt Media and to measure the impact of changes. We do not use it to train AI models, and people at Impackt Media cannot read it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Changes to your website

Impackt Media only changes your website after you approve a specific fix. We keep the previous value of every change so you can roll it back.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with our infrastructure providers (Supabase for the database, Netlify for hosting). Access tokens for connected services are encrypted at rest. When we draft content, answer your assistant questions or check AI answers, the relevant text is sent to the AI provider performing that task (such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or Perplexity) under their API terms.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any service at any time from Connections. You can also revoke access from that service's own settings, for example your Google account permissions.
  • Ask us to export or delete your data by contacting your Impackt Media account team using the contact details provided during onboarding. We delete account data within 30 days of a request.

Contact

Questions about privacy: your Impackt Media account team using the contact details provided during onboarding.
