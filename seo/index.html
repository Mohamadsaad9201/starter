Being online is not the same as being discoverable.

Customers search with a purpose. A missing answer, unclear product page or weak search snippet can leave that purpose unmet. Impackt Media brings the evidence and proposed changes into the same workspace.

A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before your agent gets to work.

Will Impackt Media guarantee first place on Google?

No. Recommendations and execution can be checked; rankings depend on many factors outside the platform. Impackt Media shows the evidence and the outcome separately.

Does every keyword need a new blog post?

No. Buying queries often belong on product or service pages. Content proposals should help a customer do something that the existing pages do not already do well.

What can it change on my website?

Supported changes depend on your connected platform and permissions. Shopify and Webflow support selected direct changes; other workflows provide reviewable copy and guided steps.
