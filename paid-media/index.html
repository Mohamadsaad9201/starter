THE PROBLEM BEHIND THE CHANNEL

A bigger budget is not a strategy.

An attractive ROAS number can hide weak margins, poor lead quality or inconsistent tracking. Start with what a customer is worth and what you can afford to acquire one.

A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before your agent gets to work.

Will Impackt Media spend money automatically?

Campaign status changes require an agency approval and enabled account controls. Resuming a campaign can restart spend; supported daily budgets are checked against the configured limit.

Can it create every campaign type?

Samaritan prepares editable Google Search campaign structures for your marketer to review. Existing connected campaigns support reporting and approved pause/resume changes. A saved proposal does not launch a new campaign.

What setup is required?

Google Ads and Meta account authorization, production API permissions and account selection are required. Your dashboard shows connection gaps instead of simulated results.
