Control should be visible.

What does an approval do?

A client decision records review of a specific version. It does not itself publish or authorize ad spend. Agency execution and platform permissions remain separate.

What happens when something stops?

The workspace shows available progress and error records. A stale job is not shown as active. Your team can inspect supported recovery options before restarting work.

How do I reach a real person?

Open Your human team to send a private business-scoped message. Specialists use an inbox with ownership and resolution controls; response arrangements are agreed during onboarding.
