A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before your agent gets to work.

Will content be published without a review?

The current workflow requires approval. Client review records a decision; agency execution and publishing permissions remain separate.

Which websites support direct article publishing?

The current direct article publishing workflow supports Shopify. Other platforms can use the prepared copy through their own publishing process.

Can I request ad copy or product copy too?

Yes. The workspace supports multiple draft types and business-specific briefs. The publishing route depends on the output and connected platform.
